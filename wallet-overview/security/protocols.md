# Protocols

**We do not assess, validate, or guarantee the safety, reliability, or correctness of integrated protocols, and their inclusion in the wallet should not be interpreted as an endorsement or security assurance.**

**The wallet interface is purely informational in nature.** Nothing displayed in the application should be considered financial advice, an investment recommendation, or an offer to invest. Users are solely responsible for their decisions and for any assets allocated to third-party protocols.

Within the interface, we use an star (⭐️) symbol next to certain protocols to indicate that their [Total Value Locked (TVL)](https://ecos.am/en/blog/what-is-total-value-locked-tvl-in-crypto?ysclid=mkfeqgy7o7743367409) may be considered systemically significant. This designation is provided solely for informational purposes and does not imply that we assume any responsibility or liability for funds placed in these protocols.

The other symbol we might use is a leaf (🌱). That's how you can indicate that this protocol is relatively new. Please, pay extra attentation to this protocols as they might not have a long traction of good perfomace.&#x20;
