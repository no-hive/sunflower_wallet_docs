# Data Politics

At the current stage, we do not collect any user data whatsoever.&#x20;

In the future, we plan to introduce an optional mechanism that allows us to determine whether a specific wallet was created through our application, solely for the purpose of providing additional bonuses to such wallets. This functionality will be entirely opt-in, and no other personal or identifiable user data will ever be collected or stored.

For this reason, we would greatly appreciate any feedback you are willing to share about our wallet. You are welcome to voluntarily tell us about yourself and your experience, including what you like or dislike about the application. This information is extremely valuable to us, as we do not receive user insights from any other sources and rely entirely on community feedback to improve the product.
