# Explain like I'm 5

**Imagine you have a piggy bank where you keep your coins.** You put money inside, and it stays there in your closet until you take it out. The money is safe and still yours, but it doesn’t do anything — it’s just sleeping and waiting.

#### What is BTCFi?

**BTCFi is like a magic piggy bank**. You put your Bitcoin inside, and while it stays safely with you, it can also help other people and earn extra coins. No person or bank is in charge — everything works by simple rules, and you can always see what’s happening and decide when to take your money back.

#### How do the “magic” extra coins appear?

The extra coins are not really magic. **They come from special systems called "protocols".** Protocols are like tiny robot businesses that work by themselves and follow rules all the time. They do simple jobs, like lending coins or helping people trade. When your coins help them do this work, they give you a little extra as a thank-you, so your coins slowly grow over time.

#### So... What is Sunflower Wallet?

Sunflower Wallet is a beginner-friendly mobile app that makes it very easy to start. We give you simple guides, a clear mobile interface, and a comfort experience, **so you don’t need any special knowledge to use a magic piggy bank that grows your coins.**
